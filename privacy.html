Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Shopnix is an educational demo marketplace. This page explains what data the site stores and how it's handled.

2. What We Store

Shopnix has no server or database. Any data you enter — account name/email, cart contents, wishlist items — is saved only in your browser's localStorage on your own device. Nothing is transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Resources

Product images are loaded from placehold.co, a placeholder image service. No other third-party scripts, analytics, or trackers are used on this site.

4. Cookies

Shopnix does not set any cookies.

5. Your Control

You can clear all locally stored Shopnix data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain, or by using your browser's developer tools to clear localStorage.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact Us page.
