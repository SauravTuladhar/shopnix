About Shopnix

Nepal's demo marketplace for everyday shopping

Our Story

Shopnix is an educational clone built to demonstrate what a modern Nepali e-commerce marketplace looks and feels like — from flash sales to category browsing to checkout. It is not affiliated with any real retailer.

Our mission is to make online shopping simple, fast and reliable, bringing thousands of products from electronics to fashion to every corner of the country.
