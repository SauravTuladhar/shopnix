Return Policy

Most items purchased on Shopnix can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by the receipt.

Refunds are processed back to your original payment method (or as account credit for Cash on Delivery orders) within 5-7 business days of us receiving the returned item.

Perishables, intimate apparel, and personal care items opened after delivery are not eligible for return.
