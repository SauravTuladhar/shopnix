Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Shopnix is a real online marketplace. This page explains what data the site stores and how it's handled.

2. What We Store

Your account name/email and order history (delivery name, phone, address, and order contents) are stored server-side in our Supabase-backed database, protected by Row Level Security so only you can read your own account and order data. Your cart and wishlist contents remain local to your own browser/device and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

3. Third-Party Resources

Product images are loaded from placehold.co, a placeholder image service. No other third-party scripts, analytics, or trackers are used on this site.

4. Cookies

Shopnix does not set any cookies.

5. Your Control

You can clear all locally stored Shopnix data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain, or by using your browser's developer tools to clear localStorage.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact Us page.
